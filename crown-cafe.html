All day, every day

Crown Cafe has been feeding Camden since before the neon sign went up. Baguettes, rolls, paninis and baps made fresh to order, home-made dinners that taste like someone's actual kitchen, and smoothies for whenever you need one.

It's a small, tidy, no-fuss room with good coffee and staff who remember what you had last time. Dine in, grab it kerbside, or have it delivered — no contact needed.

Free WiFi, two zones covered, so you can linger over that cappuccino a while longer.
