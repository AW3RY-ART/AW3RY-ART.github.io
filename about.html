About me

A little about me

A self-taught eye, shaped by the things I love.

I learned by doing, experimenting, and making plenty of mistakes along the way. That process still shapes how I work: stay curious, pay attention, and keep refining the image until it feels right.

AW3RY began in 2024 as a place to bring automotive photography and visual design together. I’m based in Łódź and always interested in meeting people with a good story to tell.
